## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** Two Sum — LeetCode

### Approach

Use two loops to check every possible pair of numbers. If the sum of two numbers equals the target, return their indices.

### Complexity

* Time: O(n²)
* Space: O(1)

### Notes

Tested locally with two test cases: one typical case and one edge case. The solution was then submitted on LeetCode.
